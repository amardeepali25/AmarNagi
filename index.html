Who I Am

About Me

Exhibition & Event Design Specialist

Hello! I'm Amarjeet Singh Nagi

I am a passionate Graphic Designer with deep expertise in 2D Exhibition and Event Design. My work revolves around creating visually compelling and strategically crafted graphics, layouts, and branding materials that elevate event spaces and exhibition experiences.

Leveraging industry-standard tools including Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe InDesign, and CorelDRAW, I transform abstract concepts into powerful visual narratives. From large-scale exhibition panels to detailed event branding, I deliver designs that captivate audiences and strengthen brand identity.

My approach combines meticulous attention to detail with a strong understanding of visual hierarchy, colour theory, and spatial composition — essential elements for any successful exhibition or event.
